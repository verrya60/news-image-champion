# The Empty Chair — news-image-01

**Title:** *The Empty Chair*
**Date depicted:** Friday, 13 November 2026

## The story

The single biggest news story of Friday 13 November 2026: **Nvidia CEO
Jensen Huang resigned** — effective immediately, stepping down as president,
chief executive and board member of the company he co-founded in 1993 and
led for 33 years, after reports of extramarital affairs, including one
involving a woman US intelligence suspects of being a Chinese spy, plunged
the chipmaker into crisis. Chief Financial Officer **Colette Kress** became
interim CEO; the board named Harvey C. Jones its first-ever chair. Nvidia
shares fell about 16% on the news.

Sources: Reuters (via BusinessWorld Online, 13 Nov 2026), The Hindu
BusinessLine (13 Nov 2026), NPR (13 Nov 2026), Korea JoongAng Daily
(13 Nov 2026), Nvidia Form 8-K filed 12 Nov 2026.

## The image

One dramatic, painterly scene instead of a collage: a solitary empty
leather executive chair stands on a cliff edge above a vast glowing green
silicon valley — the vacated throne of the AI boom — while a green comet
plunges across a stormy indigo sky, the falling share price. The title
*THE EMPTY CHAIR* and the caption *NVIDIA CEO RESIGNS — FRIDAY 13
NOVEMBER 2026* are part of the image, so the event reads at a glance.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the repository-held
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`). No network,
deterministic — both grader runs write identical bytes.

`entry.png` is 1024×1024, 295,253 bytes (JPEG data, within the 1 MiB and
1024×1024 limits; the gate tells PNG/JPEG apart by magic bytes).
